An advanced audio signal generator and Web Audio suite designed to drive **Bluetooth Speaker Passive Radiators** and **Drivers** into maximum physical vibration using targeted acoustic resonance, near-ultrasonic carrier frequencies, and amplitude modulation.
## 🎯 Target Hardware & Acoustics Principle

### 1. Passive Radiator Pneumatic Resonance (45 Hz – 60 Hz)
* The top/bottom disc on cylindrical Bluetooth speakers (Portronics, JBL, Sony) with a rubber surround is a **Passive Bass Radiator**.
* It has no internal voice coil or magnet. It moves strictly via **pneumatic air pressure** pushed inside the sealed cabinet by the internal active woofer.
* Tuning the carrier wave to **45 Hz – 60 Hz** matches the mechanical resonance of the rubber membrane, producing **maximum physical cone excursion**.

### 2. Near-Ultrasonic Tweeter Bypass (18.5 kHz – 20 kHz)
* Low frequencies are blocked from tweeter circuits by internal crossover capacitors.
* Sending high frequencies (**18.5 kHz – 20.0 kHz**) routes 100% of the energy into the tweeter voice coil.
* Human hearing drops off dramatically above 16.5 kHz, making the 18.5 kHz signal **practically inaudible** while physically vibrating the tweeter dome.

### 3. Click Transient Suppression
* Abruptly starting or stopping high/low sine waves produces audible clicking artifacts (spectral splatter).
* A **5ms – 20ms Tukey Cosine Window Taper** is applied to every burst to guarantee silent, click-free audio start and stop transitions.
